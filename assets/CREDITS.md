## Before production

These are stand-ins chosen to make the layout reviewable, not final art direction:

- **Three of the four are from the same stock shoot** (same models, same consulting room). Fine for a
  layout review, too repetitive to ship.
- **No model releases.** CC0 and CC BY cover the *copyright*, not the depicted person's likeness
  rights. Pairing these faces with the fictional patient testimonials in the mockup is acceptable for
  internal review but **must not ship**. Production needs either commissioned photography with
  releases, or licensed stock with model releases, or real community members with consent.
- `article-2.jpg` is CC BY 2.0, so it needs a visible credit if it is used anywhere public.
- The brief calls for warm, hopeful, human imagery. These read clinical. Commissioned photography of
  real community members would serve the brand far better.
