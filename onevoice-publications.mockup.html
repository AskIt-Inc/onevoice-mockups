Amyloidosis research and publications

Papers, conference abstracts and clinical guidance relevant to amyloidosis, collected in one place with the citation details intact. Each record links to the version of record at the publisher — we index this literature, we do not republish it.

What is indexed here

  • Peer-reviewed papers, with authors, source and DOI where the record holds them.
  • Conference abstracts and posters, labelled as such.
  • Consensus statements and clinical guidance.

This is a curated reading list, not a systematic review and not a complete index of the literature. Plain-language information is here.
