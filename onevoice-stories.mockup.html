This page is blocked on two client decisions that have not been answered. It was built so the decisions can be made by looking at a page rather than at a spec, and it is deliberately not linked from the review index. Nothing here is signed off.

Every storyteller below is fictional. No real community record was available, and attributing an invented story to a real person would be a false claim about that person. Only the Amyloidosis record count (143) has a documented source; the other three communities are marked as unmeasured rather than presented as data.

Decision 1

Do video and video_visuals merge into community_center?

17 nodes site-wide — 7 plus 10 — against 143 community records in this community alone. Roughly a tenth of the merged set, and the brief’s “33” was a URL count, not a node count. Either way the number does not decide it: what decides it is whether video_visuals holds diagrams rather than personal stories. Diagrams belong under Learn, not here.

Decision 4

Do social posts become embeds?

Native quoting costs no third-party request and keeps card geometry predictable. An embed is a request from a page about one condition to a social network — the concern in prompt 20. Click-to-load is the middle option, and is what the embed view below shows.

            Share your story

            The stories on this page exist because people decided the next person should not start from nothing. You do not have to be articulate, finished, or over it.

            • Your name is a separate choice from your story. Publish under your name, your first name and last initial, or anonymously.
            • A photograph is never required, and we do not add one. Stories without a photo are shown with your initials, or with nothing at all if you are anonymous.
            • You can change your mind. Ask us to unpublish or to remove your name, and we do it — you do not have to give a reason.

            We use what you send to prepare your story for this community site and to reply to you about it. We do not sell your information, share it with anyone else, or add you to any mailing list. Publishing is your decision, and nothing goes live until you have seen it.

            Three ways to start

            • Write it yourself any length
            • Talk it through with us 30 min call
            • Record a voice note we transcribe it

            Every published video and audio story carries captions and a transcript. If yours does not have them yet, we make them before it goes live.
