What to look at

One template, many communities. The colour dots switch between Amyloidosis, Sickle Cell Disease and two future indications. Only the accent colour tokens and the content change — the layout, components and markup are identical. Adding community #101 is a config file, not a redesign.

Mobile first. Every screen is designed at 390 px first: 16px+ body text, 44px+ tap targets, and a thumb-reachable bottom navigation bar.

SEO structure. Tick specs in the floating control to see the annotations — single H1 matching search intent, keyword intro above the fold, news and upcoming sessions high in the page for freshness, breadcrumbs, and Schema.org slots for NewsArticle, Event and MedicalTrial.
